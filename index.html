Tell Auntie About Your Kid

Auntie Apps turns a child’s current obsession, big idea, or wonderfully specific little world into a personalized browser game made just for them.

Here’s what’s happening

You bring the kid lore. Auntie makes it playable.

1 Tell me about your child.

Share what they love, what they imagine, and a few details that make them unmistakably them.

2 I create their game.

I turn the idea into a small personalized browser game and send you an unlisted link.

3 You test it together.

Let them play, watch what happens, and tell me honestly what delighted them, confused them, or needs work.

This is a complimentary Founding Family pilot. You are helping me test the experience before it opens to paying families. There is no cost, no posting requirement, and no expectation of a glowing review. Any request to share a quote, screenshot, or clip will come separately, and you can always say no.
