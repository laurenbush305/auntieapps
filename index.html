Tell Auntie About Your Kid

Auntie Apps turns a child’s very specific interests into a personalized browser experience. Some become playful games. Others feel more like a virtual children’s museum, a story world, a discovery room, a scavenger adventure, or a hands-on activity starter.

Here’s what’s happening

You bring the kid lore. Auntie builds the right kind of experience.

1Tell me what lights them up.

Share their current fascination, how they naturally like to play or explore, and a few unmistakably-them details.

2I shape the format around them.

The result might be a game, exhibit, story, creative prompt, movement experience, or another small browser-based world.

3You review it first.

You receive an unlisted link, decide whether it fits your family, and share honest feedback after testing.

This is a complimentary pilot. There is no cost, posting requirement, or expectation of a positive review. Any request to share a quote, image, recording, or part of the experience publicly will come separately, and you can always say no.

Have more than one kiddo? Feel free to submit a separate form for each child so each experience can be built around their own interests and personality.
