The essentials / 02

Your ballot is local. Your preparation should be, too.

Spelman women vote all over the country. Registration rules, candidates, ballot measures, voting hours, and identification requirements can differ by state and even by address.

Start with your own registration and ballot lookup, then check the election office for your jurisdiction before making a voting plan.

Georgia voters

Georgia’s general election is November 3, 2026, with advance voting scheduled October 13–30. Check official state and county election resources for the latest details before heading to the polls.
